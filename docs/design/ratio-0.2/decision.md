# Ratio workbench direction

The first view needs to answer two questions at once: what else is in this project, and what was I going to do next on this issue? The ledger study was quickest to scan. The workbench kept the selected issue’s evidence and next action within reach. The focus queue made resumption immediate, but pulled attention away from issue comparison.

The production direction uses ledger rows and an optional, source-backed issue inspector on sufficiently wide displays. Direct links continue to open the full issue. At phone widths the reading and scanning tasks take turns. Home concentrates the saved checkpoint and actionable findings. All areas share the same navigation and controls. This choice was made after viewing all three concepts at the same dimensions with the same Luma records.
